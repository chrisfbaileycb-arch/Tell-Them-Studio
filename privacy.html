The short version

During the preview there are no accounts. We do not ask for your name or email address, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Most of what you do stays in your own browser. This page explains what does reach us, and why. The studio is operated by .

What reaches our servers

  • Your handle and a session cookie. When you connect to the community catalog we store the handle you chose and set one cookie, tt_session, so the studio recognises that handle for up to 30 days. Scripts on the page cannot read it.
  • Creations you sync or publish. The audio, title, genre, lyrics or script, origin label and remix settings. Published creations are public.
  • What you ask the AI to make. Your brief, lyrics or script, and any reference image or audio you attach, are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the result. We do not keep a copy on our servers, apart from what becomes part of a creation you sync or publish.
  • Praise and reports. Praise you leave is public under your handle. For a report we keep the reason and any details you type, plus a scrambled (salted and hashed) identifier so the same report is not counted twice. We do not keep the raw value.
  • Feeds and webhooks you set up. Show details, the addresses you give us and signing secrets, which are stored encrypted.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our servers and hosting provider process your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and to limit abuse.

What stays in your browser

Your drafts, your private library, your handle and a cached copy of the public catalog are stored in your browser. We cannot see them, and clearing your browser data deletes them. Export anything you want to keep.

Cookies and third parties

  • We use one essential cookie (tt_session). There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page opens.
  • Generation requests are processed by Google under Google's own terms for its AI services.
  • Our hosting and storage providers process data on our behalf.

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep things

  • Published creations stay until you unpublish or withdraw them.
  • When you withdraw a creation it stops being available straight away, and stored copies are deleted after a grace period of up to 30 days.
  • We keep a fingerprint (a hash) of withdrawn or removed audio so the same file cannot be uploaded again.

Your choices

  • Change your handle, unpublish or withdraw a creation at any time in My Library.
  • Clear your browser data to remove everything stored on your device.
  • To ask what we hold about a handle, or to have it deleted, write to . Depending on where you live you may have further legal rights over your personal data.

Children

The studio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
