Refunds and cancelling

Last updated

Where things stand

Paid plans are not on sale yet, so nothing can be charged and there is nothing to refund. This page sets out the policy we intend to apply when plans open. It may change before then.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel a subscription at any time.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and is not renewed after that.
  • We do not charge a fee for cancelling.

Refunds

  • Not what you expected? Write to us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it. We may decline if most of that plan's credits have already been used.
  • Charged by mistake? Duplicate or mistaken charges are refunded in full.
  • One-time credit packs can be refunded within 14 days if none of their credits have been used.
  • Refunds go back to the payment method you used.

Renders that go wrong

A render that fails, or that comes back as a labeled placeholder because the AI service was unavailable, does not use credits. If credits are taken for one by mistake, tell us and we will return them.

Your legal rights

Where the law in your country gives you stronger rights, for example a statutory right to cancel, those rights apply and nothing here reduces them.
