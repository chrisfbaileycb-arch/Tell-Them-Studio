Our promises

Honest by design

AI-made music raises fair questions. Here is how we answer them.

  • Labeled, always

    Every creation carries an origin label: AI-generated, or a human voice with an AI band. The label travels with it into the showcase and into exported videos.

  • Your consent, your call

    You decide whether others may remix your work. Withdraw a published piece and it comes down, along with any remixes built on it.

  • We never hold your logins

    Publishing elsewhere is a handoff: you download and you upload, with your own account. Podcasts get a standard RSS feed that you submit yourself.

  • A kind place to share

    Everything is screened before it is listed, every public track has a report button, and work that is reported repeatedly is hidden until it has been reviewed.

Questions

Good things to ask

Is the music really made by AI?

Yes. Songs are generated with Google's Lyria models and voices with Gemini, from your description. Every creation carries a label that says so, in the studio, in the showcase and in the videos you export. If you sing over an instrumental, the label says that too.

Do I need an account?

Not during the preview. You pick a handle and start. Your drafts and library are kept in your own browser, so export anything you want to keep before clearing your browser data. Accounts that follow you across devices are planned with the paid plans.

What does it cost?

The studio is free to use during the preview. Paid plans with monthly credits are planned and are not on sale yet. See the planned pricing.

Who owns what I make?

What you write stays yours: your ideas, your lyrics, your scripts. For the generated audio, the rules on AI-made work differ from place to place and also depend on our AI provider's terms, so we do not promise exclusive ownership. Our terms of use explain what you can do with your creations.

Can I use a song or a spot for my business?

Commercial-use licensing is planned for the Pro plan. Until those terms are published, please treat what you make as personal use.

Can I make something that sounds like a famous artist?

No. The studio is for original work. It is not built to copy protected lyrics or melodies or to imitate a recognisable singer's voice, and our community rules do not allow it.

Can I put my song on Spotify or YouTube?

You can download your audio and upload it yourself, and podcasts can publish a standard RSS feed that you submit to directories. We never ask for your logins to other platforms and never post for you. Each platform has its own rules for AI-made music, so check them first.

What happens when the AI service is unavailable?

It happens sometimes. When it does, the studio tells you plainly and shows a labeled placeholder instead of passing something off as your song.
