Pricing

Pay for what you make

Every plan comes with monthly credits to spend on songs, podcasts and radio spots. Start free, and move up when you are making more.

Credits

One simple unit

A credit is what you spend to make something. Short pieces use one, longer ones a little more. Listening, exporting videos and getting help with lyrics never use credits.

A render that fails, or that comes back as a labeled placeholder because the AI service was unavailable, will not use credits.

Questions

Before you choose

When can I buy a plan?

Paid plans open once accounts and billing are ready. Until then the studio is free to use and nothing on this page will be charged. Prices and allowances may change before launch.

Do unused credits roll over?

Monthly credits reset each month. Credits from a one-time pack stay valid for twelve months.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. The refund policy has the details.

Can I use what I make for my business?

A commercial-use licence is planned for the Pro and Business plans, and its terms will be published before those plans go on sale. Free and Creator are for personal use.

Why credits instead of unlimited?

Every song, voice and spot costs real computing time. Credits let us keep the entry price low and charge fairly for what each person actually makes.
